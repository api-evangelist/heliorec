Great to meet with the STEWART consortium at Fraunhofer-Institut für Solare Energiesysteme ISE on February 2–3, where partners came together to share progress, exchange ideas, and align on next steps for developing science-based, environmentally friendly layouts for floating PV. Special thanks to Fraunhofer for organising an insightful field trip to a 1.5 MW floating solar plant on a quarry lake near Leimersheim!
