Introduction Solar PV can be deployed in three main forms: ground-mounted, rooftop, and floating. Each addresses a different combination of available surface, project scale, and consumption profile. Floating solar (FPV) is most often benchmarked against ground-mounted systems, since both are typically utility-scale and grid-connected.
